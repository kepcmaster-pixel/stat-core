🧮 Statistician's core v1.0

The absolute essentials — foundations, inference, regression & pitfalls. Hover cards for a lift.

📊 Foundations

  • Pop vs sample — parameters vs statistics; sampling distribution is the bridge.
  • Central tendency — mean (sensitive), median (robust), mode (categorical).
  • Dispersion — variance, SD, IQR, range. Always report with a measure of spread.
  • Shape & outliers — skewness, kurtosis, boxplots, z‑scores. Check before assuming normality.
📌 Law of total variance, covariance, correlation ≠ causation.

🎲 Probability & distributions

  • Bayes' theorem — prior · likelihood → posterior. Foundation of Bayesian inference.
  • Common distributions — Normal, Binomial, Poisson, Exponential, Beta, Gamma.
  • LLN & CLT — sample mean → population mean; sample mean → normal (n large).
  • Expectation & variance — linearity of expectation, var(X+Y).
⚡ Moment generating functions, transformations of variables.

📐 Inference

  • Hypothesis tests — p‑value, significance level (α), power (1−β), Type I/II errors.
  • Confidence intervals — interpretation: 95% CI captures true parameter in 95% of repeated samples.
  • t‑tests & ANOVA — one‑sample, two‑sample, paired; F‑test for variance & group means.
  • Non‑parametric — Wilcoxon, Mann‑Whitney, Kruskal‑Wallis when assumptions fail.
🔁 Bootstrap, permutation tests, likelihood ratio.

📈 Regression & modeling

  • Linear regression — OLS, assumptions: linearity, independence, homoscedasticity, normality of residuals.
  • R² & adjusted R² — goodness‑of‑fit, but can inflate with more predictors.
  • Logistic regression — log‑odds, odds ratios, interpretation of coefficients.
  • Regularization — Ridge (L2), Lasso (L1), ElasticNet for high‑dimensional data.
🧪 Interaction terms, multicollinearity (VIF), cross‑validation.

⚠️ Design & pitfalls

  • Bias & confounding — selection bias, recall bias, Simpson's paradox.
  • Randomization & blocking — reduce confounding, balance groups.
  • Sample size & power — determine before study; underpowered studies are common.
  • Multiple comparisons — Bonferroni, Benjamini‑Hochberg (FDR).
🚩 p‑hacking, overfitting, data leakage, cherry‑picking.

🧠 Bayesian & modern

  • Bayesian inference — posterior ∝ prior × likelihood; credible intervals.
  • MCMC & variational inference — computational tools for posterior approximation.
  • Hierarchical models — partial pooling, shrinkage; great for grouped data.
  • Resampling — cross‑validation, bootstrap, jackknife.
🌐 Causal inference, A/B testing, missing data (MCAR/MAR/MNAR).
must‑know GLM · survival analysis · time series · experimental design
